# Product backlog

Eighteen stories, estimated in points, assigned to one of three sprints. Priority uses MoSCoW. Acceptance criteria are written Given / When / Then and kept to two lines each, so they can be read out during the review.

Capacity note: one developer, two weeks per sprint, about 25 points available. We discovered in sprint 1 that a five point story takes a full working week once tests are counted, so the numbers below are the corrected ones after we re-estimated.

## Ordering rules we used

Must have stories are not all in sprint 1. We put the money pages first because they are the ones we can demo without explaining anything, and moved change password and the empty states to sprint 3 because nothing breaks without them.

Two stories were split during the work. The dashboard story was originally one item worth 13 points covering totals, comparison, category breakdown, recent rows, over budget list and the chart. We split the chart into US-14 and the rest stayed in US-13, which is why US-13 is still our largest estimate. CSV import was estimated at 3 points before we wrote the preview step and re-estimated to 6 after seeing how much error reporting each row needs.
